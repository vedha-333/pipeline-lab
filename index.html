THEORY & LEARNING GUIDE

What the Simulator Models

01

Pipelined Instruction Cycle

In a single-cycle CPU, each instruction must sequentially execute through Fetch, Decode, Execute, Memory, and Writeback before the next instruction begins. Pipelining divides the datapath into 5 independent stages separated by pipeline latches, allowing up to 5 instructions to be processed simultaneously.

02

Pipeline Hazards & Solutions

RAW Hazards: Resolved via Forwarding bypass wires directly from EX/MEM or MEM/WB latches to the ALU inputs.
Load-Use Hazards: Data from memory is only ready after MEM stage; requires 1 stall bubble even with forwarding.
Control Hazards: Branch resolution in EX stage incurs 2-cycle penalty for taken branches.

03

Performance & CPI Math

CPI: Total Cycles / Completed Instructions (approaches 1.0 in ideal conditions).
Throughput (IPC): Completed Instructions / Total Cycles = 1 / CPI.
Speedup: (Serial Execution Time / Pipelined Time) = (5 × N) / C.
